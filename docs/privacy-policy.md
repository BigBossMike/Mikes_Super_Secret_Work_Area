# Intro – Privacy Policy

Draft 28th October 2026. For legal review before public launch. Holds are numbered at the end.

Last updated: [Hold 1]

## 1. Who we are

Intro is run by Cavalry Software [Hold 2]. In this policy "we", "us" and "Intro" mean Cavalry Software. "You" means a person using the Intro app.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

## 2. What Intro does with your information, in short

- You set a plan. Intro asks up to 5 well-matched people if they are happy to be put forward.
- Nobody can browse or search Intro. People see you only after they agree to be put forward for your plan, or you agree for theirs.
- Chat stays locked until you meet in person and connect your phones. Only then can you swap contact details.
- Everyone is ID checked. We keep only that you are verified and your date of birth, never the ID.
- We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

## 4. Sensitive information

Cultural background is sensitive information under the Privacy Act. You can add it or leave it out. If you add it, you consent to Intro using it for matching. It is never shown to another person. You can delete it at any time in Me.

The ID check uses your face (a selfie) to match your ID document. That is biometric information. The ID check provider handles it under its own privacy policy, named in section 10. Intro never receives or stores the ID document or the selfie. Intro receives only: verified or not, and your date of birth. Intro compares your profile photo to the verification selfie to confirm the photo is you [Hold 3]. Intro keeps the result (match or no match), not the images.

## 5. Age and the ID check

Intro is for people aged 16 and over. Everyone is ID checked before they can be matched.

There are two ways to be checked: a bank check (through ConnectID) or a photo ID with a selfie. A government ID is never the only way.

If the check shows you are under 16, the account is closed and the information collected is deleted.

## 6. People aged 16 and 17, and parents

If you are 16 or 17:

- A parent or guardian must approve your account. You give us their mobile number so they can sign in and approve it.
- Your parent can choose to approve each plan before anyone is asked.
- Your parent is told when a plan is set, when you connect with someone in person, and when you report someone or are reported.
- Your parent never sees your chats, your matches before you meet, or the people who said no.
- You are only ever matched with other 16 and 17 year olds.

If you are a parent:

- We collect your mobile number and your relationship to the teen.
- You can see the teen's profile answers, their plans and their in-person connections, and the settings above.

## 7. Hide me from my contacts

This setting is on by default. It stops you being matched with anyone whose number is in your phone's contacts, and stops anyone who has your number in their contacts being matched with you.

To do this, with your permission the app reads the numbers in your contacts, turns each one into a fingerprint (a one-way hash) on your phone, and uploads only the fingerprints. The numbers, names and anything else in your contacts never leave your phone. The fingerprints are stored only for this check and only you can read them. You can turn the setting off in Me, and the fingerprints are deleted.

## 8. Location when you connect in person

When you and the other person press Connect with your phones together, the app sends each phone's location at that moment. The server checks the two phones are in the same place. It also uses Bluetooth or a QR code to check the phones are next to each other.

The location is kept with the connect record for [Hold 4] and then deleted. Only the suburb is kept with the connection. Intro does not track your location at any other time.

## 9. Chat and safety

- Messages with phone numbers, emails, social handles or links are blocked until you have connected in person. The check runs on the server.
- You can report or block anyone from a chat. Reports are reviewed by Intro's safety team, who can read the reported chat.
- Intro checks for patterns of people targeting one person (for example several people who know each other being put forward to the same person) and may hold back a match and raise a flag for the safety team.
- Intro checks plan text automatically before anyone is asked. Plans that read as dating, put someone down or single someone out are refused. For 16 and 17 year olds, so are alcohol, vapes, drugs and meeting at someone's home.
- Intro keeps a record of matches, answers and connections to run these checks.

## 11. Overseas disclosure

Your data is stored in Australia. The SMS, notification, payment and ID check services in section 10 may process limited information outside Australia, in the countries shown. We choose providers that commit to protecting personal information to a standard at least as high as the Australian Privacy Principles.

## 13. Your choices

In Me you can:

- change your answers and photo
- turn "can be put forward" off (pause)
- turn "hide me from my contacts", "keep my school out", "keep my workplace out" and discreet notifications on or off
- delete your cultural background
- sign out
- delete your account

Discreet notifications: when on, every notification says only "Intro: you have an update".

## 14. Access, correction and deletion

You can see and change almost everything we hold about you in the app. To ask for a copy of your information, correct something you cannot change in the app, or delete your account, contact us (section 18). We respond within 30 days.

When you delete your account we delete your profile, photo, plans, matches, chats, contact details and fingerprints. We keep the records in section 12 that we must or that protect other people. People you connected with in person keep the contact details you shared with them.

## 15. Security

- Everything is encrypted between your phone and our servers.
- Access rules run in the database itself, so each person can read only what they are allowed to, even if the app is tampered with.
- Staff access is limited to the safety team and only for reports, flags and support.
- We never store your ID document, selfie, payment card or contact list.

## 16. Data breaches

If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we notify the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

## 17. Children under 16

Intro is not for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 16, we close it and delete the information.

## 18. Complaints and contact

Privacy questions, requests and complaints: [Hold 8].

We acknowledge a complaint within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the OAIC at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

We update this policy when Intro changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For a change that affects how we use your information, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
