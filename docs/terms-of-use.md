By creating an account or using Intro you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy.

## 2. What Intro is

Intro helps people meet for a plan. The following is how it works:

- You set a plan (an "objective"), for example a fifth player for football or someone for a midweek dinner.
- Intro asks up to 5 well-matched people if they're happy to be put forward. You see who they are only if they say yes.
- Chat stays locked to plain messages. Phone numbers, emails, socials and links unlock only when you meet in person and press Connect with your phones together.

Intro is not a dating service. Plans for dating are not allowed.

## 3. Who can use Intro

- You must be 16 or older.
- You must pass the ID check. During the pilot the check uses the date of birth you enter [Hold 3].
- If you are 16 or 17, a parent or guardian must approve your account. You are only ever matched with other 16 and 17 year olds.
- If you are 18 or older, you are only ever matched with adults, inside the age range you set and the other person sets.
- One account per person. Accounts can't be shared, sold or transferred.
- You must be in a city where Intro runs. Brisbane for now.

## 4. Your account

- You sign in with your mobile number. Keep your phone and sign-in codes to yourself.
- Your details must be true: your own first name, your own photo of your face, your own date of birth.
- You are responsible for what happens on your account.
- Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

## 5. Rules for plans, chat and meeting

You must not use Intro to:

- Look for dates, hook-ups or anything sexual.
- Put anyone down, mock, bully, threaten or harass anyone.
- Single out or target a particular person, school, class or workplace.
- Get around the chat lock, for example by spelling out a phone number or social handle.
- Arrange anything illegal, or alcohol, vapes or drugs with anyone under 18.
- Meet a 16 or 17 year old at a private home.
- Sell anything, recruit, advertise or ask for money.
- Pretend to be someone else, or use a photo that isn't you.
- Set up or work with other accounts to aim requests at the same person.
- Copy, scrape or collect information about other people on Intro.

Intro checks plans before anyone is asked and may refuse a plan that breaks these rules.

## 6. Safety

Intro checks ages and keeps chat locked until you meet. It does not run criminal record checks and can't promise anyone's behaviour.

- Meet somewhere public the first time.
- Tell someone you trust where you're going and when.
- It's fine to bring a friend. Say so in the plan ("Me + 1 other").
- Leave if anything feels wrong.
- Report or block anyone from the chat. Reports go to Intro's safety team.
- If someone is in danger, call 000 first.

## 7. Parents of 16 and 17 year olds

- A parent or guardian approves the teen's account by signing in with the mobile number the teen entered.
- The parent can choose to approve each plan before anyone is asked, be told when the teen meets someone, and see the place and time of a meet-up.
- The parent can pause the teen's account.
- The parent never sees the teen's chats.
- A parent must be the teen's parent or legal guardian, and 18 or older.
